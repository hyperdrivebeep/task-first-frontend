Persona and journey notes:
- Viewers expect rich video in any app and want to focus on what they are watching, not on the player.
- Viewers pick a title from outside the app, expect playback to start at once, and expect resume to pick up where they stopped.
- Viewers open controls or supporting tabs mid-playback only briefly and want to return to the picture quickly.
- Viewers need control over their experience and do not expect to be pulled into immersive video without warning.
- Viewers with several profiles expect playback and history to belong to the profile they picked.
- Journey: loading screen only if slow, then playback starts, then exit, then a detail view with a resume option.
