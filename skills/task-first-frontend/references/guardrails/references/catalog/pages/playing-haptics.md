Persona and journey notes:
- Game players receive custom gameplay haptics, which the page treats as the main case for long or custom haptics.
- App users are focused on a task, so long or frequent haptics can distract them, and they expect to be able to turn haptics off.
- People rely on familiar haptic patterns from standard controls and recognize them by repeated use.
