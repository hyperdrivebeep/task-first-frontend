## What is excluded from universal rules

Apple branding and legal wording, system services and APIs, native-only layout anatomy, platform sizing tables, unsupported-device matrices and exact hardware-specific timing/geometry stay in the candidate exclusion sections. Their presence in the source is recorded; exclusion from this portable skill does not exempt a native product from its applicable requirements.

Index and resource-only content contributes to coverage, not extra requirements. Conditional principles from payments, health, identity, sensing, vehicle or spatial pages are adopted only with their task condition preserved. The source-only gounmaeum paper check does not exercise those capabilities.
