## Disclosure controls

URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/disclosure-controls

Universal rules:
- MUST place the most-used controls at the top of the disclosure hierarchy, always visible | Best practices
- MUST hide advanced functionality by default | Best practices
- MUST point a disclosure triangle inward (leading edge) when hidden and down when visible, and toggle it on click or tap | Disclosure triangles
- MUST give a disclosure triangle a descriptive label that says what is hidden or shown, such as "Advanced Options" | Disclosure triangles
- MUST point a disclosure button down when hidden and up when visible | Disclosure buttons
- MUST place a disclosure button near the content it shows and hides, with a clear relationship to the revealed choices | Disclosure buttons
- MUST NOT use more than one disclosure button in a single view | Disclosure buttons

Apple-specific (excluded): Keynote, Finder, and macOS Save sheet examples; SwiftUI DisclosureGroup; AppKit disclosure and pushDisclosure; tvOS, watchOS, and visionOS support notes; macOS has no extra considerations; disclosure controls are not supported in tvOS or watchOS.
