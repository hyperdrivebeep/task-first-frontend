Persona and journey notes:
- A direct-item buyer expects to buy only the selected item; a cart buyer expects express checkout to cover the whole prepared basket. Existing cart contents must not silently change either intent.
- A buyer choosing sizes, delivery destinations, pickup locations, or optional messages needs those inputs at a stage the payment interface actually supports.
- A guest buyer can finish first and register later using already supplied details.
- A buyer comparing delivery or reviewing costs needs clear methods, charges, discounts, merchant identity, and the distinction between current, pending, and future amounts.
- A buyer encountering invalid data needs tolerant parsing and specific recovery guidance; a cancellation or timeout must stop the pending attempt while allowing a fresh attempt.
- A subscriber needs the recurring schedule, trial transition, initial fees, and immediate charge explained before approval and summarized at approval.
- A donor benefits from both suggested amounts and a custom-amount option within the supported donation flow; the source's example amounts are not requirements.
- Conditional transfer applies only where the target offers the described payment-review, cross-device, shipping, subscription, or donation capability. These notes do not determine payment-provider contracts, donation eligibility, or legal permission for a charge.
- Missing source material includes several legal and donation links, button artwork/captions, and blank or merged-looking button-size table cells. These are not reconstructed or converted into universal values.
- All brand/button/trademark instructions remain excluded. General checkout recovery does not imply the Apple-specific rule that every payment button must remain enabled.
