Persona and journey notes:
- People hold the device partially folded like a book, placed down on a surface, or standing on its edges; controls stay on the side so the experience stays consistent between poses.
- Controls that disappear or shift dramatically are harder to find and track; keeping them in familiar positions helps people learn how the app works right away.
- Badges and other status items are seen at a glance, so they stay visible longer than plain items.
