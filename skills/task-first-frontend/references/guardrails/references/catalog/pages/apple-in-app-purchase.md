Persona and journey notes:
- A prospective buyer needs to experience value, understand the product, and see the complete price before purchasing. Limited free access is a consideration, not a mandate to provide every paid feature free.
- A person whose account cannot make payments needs an understandable unavailable store rather than a broken purchasing path.
- A purchaser and a family recipient may share access while needing different explanations of how it was obtained.
- A person with a missing or problematic purchase needs identification, useful help, and a clear refund route; remedies and retention content must not become barriers.
- A new subscriber, an existing subscriber from another channel, and a subscriber returning on another device need different paths. Sign-in or restoration prevents needless duplicate purchases.
- Introductory offers and free trials must be distinguished from standard recurring charges; comparisons should not obscure the actual billing total.
- A person redeeming an offer may already subscribe, may be upgrading, or may not have opened the app yet. The post-redemption experience needs to reflect the resulting access.
- Offer-code distribution guidance applies only when the target supports comparable one-time and campaign codes. Apple-specific character sets and redemption locations are not generalized.
- A subscriber managing cost needs renewal visibility and accessible upgrade, downgrade, and cancellation; a return offer is only a consideration and this note authorizes no messages or external outreach.
- A person on a small or constrained-input device still needs the material offer information, an understandable comparison, and a way back to free content. The two small-screen option layouts are alternatives, not simultaneous requirements.
- Missing source material includes marketing/business links, product-rule links, Family Sharing notification links, promotional-image references, and screenshot content. No absent requirements or visual details are reconstructed.
